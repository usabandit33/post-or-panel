Answers homeowners actually ask

Is a leaning fence a post or a panel?

Often the posts. RKC Wood Care Pros writes that Kansas City leans usually trace to posts set shallower than the 30–36 inch frost line they publish, and that sound panels can stay. Probe at the soil line. Soft through the body is a post. Missing pickets on a solid post are a panel or fastener job.

What should the quote separate?

Post resets, panel swaps, a gate re-hang, and a full replacement. On its own repair page RKC publishes roughly $150–$300 for one post reset and about $80–$180 for a matched panel. A per-foot number on a repair is a reason to ask what failed.

Permit or not?

City by city. Total Home Fence & Deck’s public guide says Overland Park requires a permit for any new fence and that Olathe exempts fences under 7 feet. That is their write-up, not a city stamp. Kansas City, Missouri ordinances are in the municipal code library. A lot in Kansas is a different city’s rule.

Who calls 811?

Ask before anyone digs. New post holes can hit public utilities and private sprinkler lines. Put the locate on the written scope.

How to use the list

  1. Photograph posts at the soil line and any downed panels.
  2. Probe each post. Count soft posts separately from broken boards.
  3. Ask if failed posts come out and go back about 36 inches, or only get straightened.
  4. Ask for post, panel, and full-replacement lines on the same quote.
  5. Ask who calls 811 and whether that city wants a permit for this scope.
  6. Call a shop from the Kansas City page. Numbers are copied from each shop’s site.
