Is the quote a post reset, a panel, or a rebuild?

A leaning Kansas City fence is often a few posts, not a whole new run. Ask which one the price is for before you sign.

Shops on the city page publish a phone on their own site. Ranges below are copied from those pages, not from this directory.

What to ask on the walk

  1. How many posts are soft or leaning, versus how many panels are only missing boards?
  2. Will failed posts be pulled and reset below the local frost line, or only re-plumbed?
  3. Is the number a panel swap, a section rebuild, or a full replacement?
  4. Who calls 811 before digging, and does this city want a fence permit for a new post or a new run?

RKC Wood Care Pros publishes a single post reset around $150–$300 and a material-matched panel around $80–$180 on its own repair page. That is their range, not a metro price.
