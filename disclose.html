Post Or Panel is a homeowner checklist for one decision: reset the post, replace the panel, or rebuild the run. It is not a contractor, not an insurer, and not a permit office.

The customer never pays this directory. Shops are not charged a listing fee on the pages you see here.

Phones and the two inboxes named on the city page were copied from the shop’s own website on 6 October 2026. If a number changes, the shop’s site wins. Reply to the operator contact and the listing can come down.

Price ranges are attributed to the page that published them. They are not a quote and not a metro average.

Governing law for this first-metro checklist is Missouri, because the city page is Kansas City, Missouri. The metro crosses into Kansas. Overland Park, Olathe, and other Kansas cities set their own fence rules. This page is not legal advice and does not create a contract.

Storm, rot, and frost-heave coverage is a question for the homeowner policy and the adjuster. Gradual rot is often treated differently from a sudden wind or tree strike. Confirm on the policy.
